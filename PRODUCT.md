# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Thai and international researchers, academics, students, and lecturers looking to submit and present mathematics and mathematics education research.

## Product Purpose
A clean, official, and welcoming landing page for the national academic conference (CSRM) hosted by Pibulsongkram Rajabhat University (PSRU), facilitating registration and submission.

## Brand Personality
Academic, Professional, Trustworthy. Grounded in PSRU's green and gold identity with high readability, clean spacing, and clear presentation.

## Anti-references
- Classic AI slop (gradient text, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section, identical grid card layouts without variation).
- Saturated SaaS template (light gray text on off-white, poor contrast, low legibility).
- Obsolete academic text dumps (pure unstyled tables, non-responsive layouts).

## Design Principles
1. **Academic Authority**: Simple, clear layouts that display information cleanly and professionally.
2. **High Readability First**: Solid text colors with outstanding contrast (WCAG AA compliant), avoiding gray-on-white or white-on-gold.
3. **PSRU Identity**: Elegant use of PSRU's corporate emerald green and warm gold as accents and solid typographic elements.
4. **No AI Scaffolding**: Avoid generic SaaS eyebrows, side-stripes, and gradient text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast (WCAG AA minimum 4.5:1 for body copy).
- Responsive layouts for tablets, mobile screens, and desktops.
- Reduced motion support for animations.
